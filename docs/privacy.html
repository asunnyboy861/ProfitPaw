Privacy Policy

ProfitPaw — effective October 13, 2026

ProfitPaw ("we", "our") is built on a simple privacy promise: your business numbers belong to you. This policy explains what the ProfitPaw iOS app does and does not collect.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • No personal information such as your name, contacts, or precise location.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data. There is nothing to sell — we collect almost nothing.

What the App Stores on Your Device

  • Profit snapshots — computed analytics cached locally so the app and its widget work offline.
  • Settings and preferences — stored in standard iOS storage.
  • API keys you enter (optional "Bring Your Own Key") — stored only in the iOS Keychain, sent only to the endpoint you configure. We never see these keys.
  • A random device identifier generated on first launch, used solely as a rate-limiting identity if you use our optional cloud AI relay. It is not linked to your identity.

Optional Cloud AI Processing

  • On-device AI (default) — daily questions are answered by Apple Intelligence on your device. Your data never leaves the device for these features.
  • Invoice scanning & deep diagnosis (optional) — if you use these cloud features, an aggregated, PII-free summary of numbers (or the invoice photo you explicitly choose) is sent to the AI service configured for your account. Invoice photos are processed solely to extract item costs and are deleted within 24 hours; they are never used for training.
  • Your own API key — if configured, requests go directly from your device to your chosen provider under that provider's privacy policy.

Store Data

When you connect a Shopify store via the ProfitPaw web dashboard, order and cost data is processed by the web service under its own agreement to compute your profit analytics. The iOS app receives only aggregated, read-only results — never customer personal data.

Children

ProfitPaw is a business tool and is not directed at children under 17.

Your Rights

You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of any server-side data associated with your store connection, contact us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update it here with a new effective date.
