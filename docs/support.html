Getting Started

  • Try the Demo Store — the app opens with a fully functional demo dataset so you can explore every feature instantly. All numbers are produced by the real profit engine.
  • Connect your real store — connect your Shopify store on the ProfitPaw web dashboard; it generates a read-only aggregation endpoint you can paste in Settings → Data.
  • Widget — long-press your Home Screen → + → search "ProfitPaw" to add the Real Profit widget (Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the iOS app really free?

Yes. ProfitPaw for iOS is a free, read-only companion dashboard. There are no purchases inside the app — plans are managed on the web dashboard via Shopify Billing. The app shows your current plan read-only.

How is "real profit" calculated?

Net profit = revenue − discounts − refunds − COGS − shipping labels − payment fees − total account ad spend − your custom costs. Refunds also return product costs. Ad spend uses full account totals, never attribution estimates. See Settings → "How we calculate profit" inside the app for the full methodology.

Why does the trust badge say 95% or "pending verification"?

That's the data trust feature working. Coverage = collected ad spend ÷ your ad accounts' total spend. Below 97% we mark the day as pending verification instead of showing numbers we can't stand behind.

Does the AI cost anything?

Daily questions run on-device with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26+) — free, unlimited, private. You can optionally add your own API key in Settings → AI Settings for unlimited cloud AI; your key is stored in the iOS Keychain and billed only by your provider.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Plans are managed on the ProfitPaw web dashboard through Shopify Billing — uninstalling the Shopify app stops billing immediately, and cancellation is one tap. The iOS app never processes payments.
